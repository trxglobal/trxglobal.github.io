Why rent energy

No energy? The network burns your TRX

Every USDT-TRC20 transfer calls a smart contract and consumes energy. There are three ways to cover it — renting is the cheapest and most flexible.

Burn TRX

  • TRX is deducted automatically — the most expensive option
  • Sending to a new address doubles the energy and the cost
  • Costs add up fast for frequent transfers and sweeps

Stake TRX

  • Requires locking up a large amount of TRX
  • Unstaking takes about 14 days
  • Fixed allocation, inflexible when volume changes

Rent energy Best

  • Pay per transfer, from 1.7 TRX per 65,000 energy
  • No capital locked, no staking
  • Delivered in ~3 s, reclaimed automatically

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is TRON energy and why rent it?

Smart-contract operations on TRON, such as sending USDT (TRC20), consume energy. Without energy, the network burns TRX from your wallet instead. Renting energy costs far less, saving up to about 80% on fees.

Do I need to share my private key?

No. Energy is delegated to your address through TRON's native resource delegation. Only your public address is needed — never a private key, seed phrase or wallet approval.

How fast is delivery?

Orders are processed automatically 24/7 and delegated in about 3 seconds on average. Failed orders are automatically retried or refunded.

How much energy does a USDT transfer need?

About 65,000 energy (one unit) to an address that already holds USDT, and about 130,000 energy (two units) to an address that has never held USDT.

I rented energy — why was TRX still burned?

Usually one of two reasons: the recipient has never held USDT, so the transfer needed twice the energy; or your address ran out of bandwidth. Each address gets free daily bandwidth, and once it is used up a small amount of TRX is burned for bandwidth.

Can I use it once without signing up?

Yes. With instant rental, send a fixed amount of TRX or USDT (TRC20) and energy is delegated to the paying address within seconds. A free account gets lower prices and order history.

Do transfer packs expire?

Transfer packs never expire. Time-based plans allow unlimited delegations during the chosen period (1 hour or 24 hours).
