Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. About this site

TRX Global (trxglobal.github.io, the “Site”) is an introduction and referral page for the TRXAPI energy rental service. The Site does not offer account sign-up, log-in, top-up or ordering. Those actions happen on the official TRXAPI websites (www.trxapi.io, login.trxapi.io) and are governed by their own privacy policy and terms.

2. Information we collect

The Site never asks for personal details, private keys, seed phrases or passwords. Visiting the Site may generate the following data:

  • Hosting logs: the Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Advertising and conversion measurement: the Site may use Google Ads conversion tracking (gtag.js) to count sign-up and log-in clicks after an ad click. Google may collect device and browsing information via cookies or similar technologies.
  • Referral parameters: if you arrive from an ad, parameters such as gclid and utm_* are stored temporarily in your browser for the current session and appended to sign-up / log-in links to measure campaign performance.

3. How we use it

This data is used only to measure advertising performance and improve the Site. We do not sell or rent visitor information.

4. Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages: GitHub General Privacy Statement
  • Google Ads: How Google uses information from ads. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings.
  • TRXAPI: please review the terms on trxapi.io before signing up or using the service.

5. Your choices

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to browse the Site.
